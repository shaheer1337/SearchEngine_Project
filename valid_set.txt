Query 5 (Title-boost demonstration)
------------------------------------------------------------
Query:
cse department

Expected Top-5:
1) CSE department of HKUST
   https://hitcslj.github.io/TestPages/ust_cse.htm
2) Test page
   https://hitcslj.github.io/TestPages/testpage.htm
3) UG
   https://hitcslj.github.io/TestPages/ust_cse/UG.htm
4) PG
   https://hitcslj.github.io/TestPages/ust_cse/PG.htm
5) The Big Clock (1948)
   https://hitcslj.github.io/TestPages/Movie/261.html

Why this demonstrates title boost:
- The query terms "cse" and "department" appear directly in the page title
  "CSE department of HKUST", so that page receives additional title-match score.
- In this system, final score combines:
  (1) cosine similarity from body terms with tf*idf/max_tf, plus
  (2) extra title-match bonuses for term/phrase matches in the title.
- This causes the exact title-relevant page to rank clearly above others.

Live demo commands (with and without title boost):
  java -cp .:htmlparser.jar:lib/jdbm-1.0.jar SearchMain "cse department"
  java -cp .:htmlparser.jar:lib/jdbm-1.0.jar SearchMain --no-title-boost "cse department"
